
# Energise

Industrial Energy & Process Intelligence Platform for Indian Manufacturing SMEs.

Energise helps manufacturing businesses understand energy consumption, identify operational inefficiencies, anticipate potential issues, and discover opportunities to reduce energy costs and emissions without compromising production quality.

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: React, Vite
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js
- Database: MongoDB
- Machine Learning: Python, FastAPI, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn
